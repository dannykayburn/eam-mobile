Breaks §4.2, and it cannot be styled around. §4.2 says the bottom nav is “visible while browsing; hidden entirely the moment any record is open” — a binary. This frame has a list and an open record at once, so both are true. Proposed: the exclusivity is a Compact-width space compromise; at Expanded, band 1 persists always and the record nests in bands 2–3 rather than replacing the shell. §4.2's three-item membership is unchanged.

Browse vs execute — where this pane stops. The detail pane shows the record and its header actions. Start Work promotes to full width (frame 3), dropping the list so band 2 can become the step rail. A workflow step is a deliberately focused surface (§14); squeezing it beside a list would both crowd it and produce the forbidden fourth band. Also note the selected row is a state portrait does not have — rotating in has to choose one and rotating out has to discard it (§20).

Why R2, and the argument is architectural. §29 made the rail's contents configuration-driven and unbounded — a tab can be placed twice, More's membership is configuration not definition (WO_MORE_TABS), and a UDS is a step (isStepKind()). A workflow of 8 steps plus 4 More entries is legitimately authorable today. A vertical column holds any number; flip to R1 and note it has already had to put More behind a dropdown to fit — the collapsed-rail compromise returning under another name, at only 8 entries.

Two details these frames settle. The timer pill (§14.2, today in .step-rail-right) sits at the foot of band 2, because it belongs to the record rather than to the app. And the action bar stays horizontal at the foot of band 3 rather than standing up into a column — height is abundant at Expanded width, so §8.4 is untouched and the portrait pattern carries over unchanged. A rotated-phone analysis would have stood it up; a tablet does not need that.

One reduction worth noticing: on Expanded the rail is always expanded, so the collapsed pill, its chevron and the expand/collapse state stop existing entirely.
