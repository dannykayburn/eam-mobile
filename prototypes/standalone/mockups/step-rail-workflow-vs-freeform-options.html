What this compares: the persistent .step-rail bar (always visible at the top of every WO workflow screen) in its two real states — Free Form / §11 fallback (WO 20450, no configured workflow, flat ungated rail) vs. a real configured workflow (WO 19257 Breakdown, 5 gated steps; WO 19831 PM, 4 gated steps, shown where the WO Type's color matters). Today the only difference is the segments row and the map's icon shape — this file explores making that split read more clearly, and separately, whether the WO Type's own admin-set icon color should paint onto this rail at all. Ordered left→right, safest→loudest, with a resolution at the end.

4 — Type icon + 8px color dot
Contained risk — color surface is ~8px
A small monochrome Type icon sits in the rail's right slot with a tiny colored dot riding it — the WO Type's own admin-set color, at its rawest, unclamped. Free Form gets the same icon slot with a neutral gray dot (no type, no color) instead of an empty gap. Two real Types shown below using their actual admin-picked colors, including one that's frankly too loud — that's the point, this is the risk made visible.

5 — Icon badge, tinted background
Contained risk — color surface is a 24px badge
Same idea, slightly bigger and more legible surface: a 24px rounded badge whose background tints ~14% toward the Type's color, icon glyph stays plain ink (never colored itself, so it's always readable). Free Form gets the same badge shape in flat neutral gray. Still the same raw unclamped admin colors as Option 4 — the badge shape reads calmer than a bare dot even at the same hue, but a truly loud color still shows through.

6 — Full-width top-edge stripe
Higher risk — shown deliberately for contrast, not recommended as-is
The Type's raw color paints a 3px stripe across the entire top edge of the rail — always in view while scrolling, not a small tag. This is the "loud" end of the spectrum: even a merely-bright color reads as a persistent colored line across every screen for that WO Type, and a truly bad admin pick (below) is hard to miss in a way none of the earlier options are.
