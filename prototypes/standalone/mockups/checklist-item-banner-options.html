Checklist item banner — the scroll anchor

Each item gets a fixed-height band carrying its number in mono, so the snap has something visible to land. Scroll forward and watch the next banner get drawn up; scroll back and pull the previous one down. Behaviour is live and is the real engine — velocity-triggered landing, aborts on touch, and item 05 is deliberately long in every frame so you can confirm that scrolling inside an item does not snap. Frames differ only in what the band carries.
