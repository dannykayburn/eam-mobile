Closes the §20 row “Equipment Photo — Equipment RV’s own header slot still unbuilt.” The photo mechanic is already built and locked on WO Record View (§7.5: 44px tile → full-screen viewer → Camera/Library/File picker). Unsettled: where it goes on Equipment RV, and what it shows with no photo set.
Scroll is real in every frame, replicating the shared mechanics exactly: .scrolled at scrollTop > 40, released at < 10; .rec-status-row animates 58px → 0 over .22s. Status and the Organization pill already share that row, so they always vanish together — the only open question is what the photo does.
Your lean — photo disappears with status — is A2. It’s drawn beside A1 (photo persists, shrunk) so you can feel both. The badge at the top of each frame reports live collapse state.
